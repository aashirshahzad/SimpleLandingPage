Usage and Precautions

Know Your Limits

Dosage is crucial. Weigh your magic mushrooms with a precise scale and start low, especially for beginners. A microdose (0.1-0.5g) can be a gentle introduction. A low dose (0.5-2g) offers a mild experience, while moderate (2-3.5g) and high doses (3.5g+) can be intense. Remember, varying strains have different potencies, so adjust accordingly.
